Personally Ranking My Top Three General Motors Engines + Specifications



Number 1: The LT4


The LT4 engine by General Motors is a 650-horsepower 6.2 liter supercharged V8, that is used in the Chevrolet Corvette C7 ZO6. This is my favorite overall for the low-end torque from the supercharger and high rev limit of 6,600 RPM.

Number 2: The LS3


The LS3 engine by General Motors is a 430-horsepower 6.2 liter naturally-aspirated V8, that is used in the Chevrolet Corvette C6 Coupe and Convertible. This is my second favorite as the engine sounds quite awesome and is quite a reliable engine compare to other GM V8s.

Number 3: The L8T



Last however not least, the L8T engine by General Motors is a 401-horsepower 6.6 liter V8 engine, which is used on Chevrolet Silverados. It boasts 464 foot-pounds of torque at 5,600 RPM, and is incredibly efficient for commerical trucks due to the torque the engine carries.
